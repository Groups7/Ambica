Pearl Buying Guide

Everything you need to know to choose the perfect pearls. From understanding quality factors to caring for your treasures.

Quality Factors

How to Choose Perfect Pearls

Lustre

  • Mirror-like Reflection: The best pearls have a deep, bright reflection that seems to glow from within.
  • Sharp vs. Dull: Look for clear, sharp reflections rather than cloudy or milky surfaces.
  • Surface Depth: High-quality pearls have multiple layers of nacre (mother-of-pearl).

Surface Quality

  • Clean Surface: Minimal spots, blemishes, or imperfections on the pearl surface.
  • Smooth Texture: Free from pits, cracks, or organic irregularities.
  • Even Color: Consistent coloration without obvious variations or patches.

Shape

  • Round Pearls: Classic, most valuable, and perfect for traditional jewellery.
  • Off-Round: Slightly irregular shapes, unique character, good value.
  • Baroque Pearls: Irregular, artistic shapes with distinctive personality.
  • Drop & Button: Small round pearls perfect for accents and detailed work.

Size

  • Measured in Millimeters: Pearl size is measured by diameter in millimeters.
  • Common Sizes: 6-7mm for small accents, 7-9mm for standard pieces, 9mm+ for statement pearls.
  • Weight Matters: Larger pearls are rarer and more valuable, but consider comfort and design.
Types

Understanding Pearl Varieties

South Sea Pearls

  • Origin: Saltwater oysters, primarily from China, Japan, and Australia.
  • Characteristics: Round shape, high lustre, typically white or cream.
  • Best For: Classic jewellery, investment pieces.

Freshwater Pearls

  • Origin: Freshwater mussels, mainly from China and the US.
  • Characteristics: Various shapes, softer lustre, wide color range.
  • Best For: Fashion jewellery, casual wear, affordable luxury.

Cultured vs Natural

  • Cultured Pearls: 99% of market, human-assisted growth process, consistent quality.
  • Natural Pearls: Extremely rare (1 in 10,000), formed without human intervention, very expensive.
  • Ambica's Promise: We only sell authentic cultured pearls with full transparency.
Care

Pearl Care & Maintenance

Daily Care

Put pearls on last when dressing to avoid contact with cosmetics and perfumes. Wipe gently with a soft cloth after each wear.

Cleaning

Use only mild soap and water for cleaning. Never use ultrasonic cleaners or harsh chemicals. Dry thoroughly before storing.

Storage

Store in a soft pouch or fabric-lined box. Keep away from other jewellery to prevent scratching. Avoid airtight containers.

Restringing

Have pearls restrung every 2-3 years with knots between each pearl. This prevents loss if the string breaks.

Avoid

Keep away from chemicals, chlorine, and extreme temperatures. Remove before swimming, showering, or exercising.

Crafting Pearl Stories Since 2004

Ambica Pearls & Jewellers began with a simple mission: to bring authentic, beautiful pearl jewellery to Hyderabad at honest prices. What started as a small showroom on Lower Tank Bund has grown into three beloved stores across the city.

For over two decades, we have served generations of families, helping them celebrate weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, and everyday moments with the timeless elegance of pearls.

Our commitment remains unchanged: real pearls, transparent pricing, and exceptional service. Every piece that leaves our store carries our promise of quality and authenticity.

Craftsmanship

The Art of Pearl Jewellery

Every piece of Ambica jewellery is handcrafted by skilled artisans who have inherited the centuries-old art of pearl stringing and jewellery making. Our craftsmen understand that each pearl is unique and treat it with the respect it deserves.

From selecting the perfect pearls to stringing them with precision and finishing with hallmarked clasps, every step is done with meticulous care. We use traditional techniques combined with modern quality standards to create pieces that last generations.

Our workshop at Lower Tank Bund is where the magic happens. Visit us to see our craftsmen at work and understand the love that goes into every piece.
